Frequently Asked Questions

What is salary after tax?

Salary after tax is the amount of money you keep after taxes and payroll deductions are subtracted from your gross salary. This is often called net pay or take-home pay.

What is gross salary?

Gross salary is the total amount you earn before any taxes or payroll deductions are taken out. It is the salary number usually shown in a job offer or employment contract.

What is take-home pay?

Take-home pay is the amount of money you actually receive after deductions. It may be lower than gross salary because of federal tax, state tax, Social Security, Medicare, and other withholdings.

How do I calculate salary after tax?

To estimate salary after tax, subtract federal income tax, state income tax, Social Security, and Medicare from your gross salary. Some calculators also estimate monthly take-home pay by dividing annual net income by 12.

Why is my paycheck smaller than my salary?

Your paycheck is smaller because salary figures are usually listed before taxes. Once payroll deductions and tax withholdings are applied, the amount you actually receive is lower.

Does this calculator include federal income tax?

Yes. A salary after tax calculator should estimate federal income tax because it applies nationwide. Federal tax rates vary depending on filing status and income level.

Does this calculator include state income tax?

Yes. State income tax is estimated based on the selected state page. Some states have no state income tax, while others apply flat or progressive tax rules.

Does this calculator include Social Security and Medicare?

A take-home pay estimate typically considers payroll taxes such as Social Security and Medicare, since these deductions are common in U.S. wage income.

Why does take-home pay differ by state?

Take-home pay differs by state mainly because state income tax rules differ. For example, a salary in Texas may produce different net income than the same salary in California.

Which states have no income tax?

Some states do not charge personal state income tax. Even in those states, federal tax and payroll taxes still reduce your final take-home pay.

Is this calculator accurate?

This calculator is designed for quick estimation and salary comparison. Actual tax withholding may differ depending on benefits, deductions, credits, local taxes, and employer payroll settings.

Can I use this calculator to compare job offers?

Yes. Comparing net pay by state is one of the most useful reasons to use a salary after tax calculator, especially if you are deciding between offers in different locations.

Can I use this calculator for relocation planning?

Yes. If you are considering moving to a different state, an after-tax salary estimate can help you compare how much income you may actually keep.

No. This site provides general salary estimation tools and informational content only. It should not be considered legal, financial, or tax advice.
